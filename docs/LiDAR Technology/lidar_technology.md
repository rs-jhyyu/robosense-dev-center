# LiDAR Technology

This section covers the product and technology background of RoboSense LiDAR:
scanning principles, point cloud characteristics, coordinate systems, and the
specifications of each product line.

This section includes:

- [LiDAR Terminology](lidar_terminology.md) — the terms used across the
  datasheets: the dToF ranging principle, accuracy vs. precision, field of
  view, angular resolution, range capability, and intensity.
- [Common Phenomena Explained](common_phenomena.md) — phenomena you may run
  into in the field, such as point cloud mirroring, high-reflectivity
  blooming, glass detection, and window contamination, and whether each one
  is a fault or normal optics.

For connecting and running a LiDAR, see [Operation Guides](../Getting Started/intro.md).
For product manuals and datasheets, see
[Manual Resources](https://www.robosense.cn/resources).
